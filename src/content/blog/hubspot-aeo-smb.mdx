AEO means Answer Engine Optimization: improving how a business is represented in AI-generated answers. For a small team, I would begin with the questions buyers ask and the information they need to judge your work.

HubSpot AEO can help monitor that visibility. Whether it is worth using depends on what you will do with the findings and how you will assess the result.

## What HubSpot AEO measures

HubSpot's [current AEO setup guide](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/seo/set-up-and-analyze-ai-visibility) describes tracking prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. It reports brand visibility, competitors and citations, with recommendations for improvement. Data collection begins after setup, and tracked prompts run daily.

These results describe the prompts and engines being tracked. A brand mention, a citation linking to your site and a visit to that site are different observations. Review them separately before connecting them to leads or revenue.

Content work still needs someone to choose the changes, check the facts and maintain the pages. A monitoring dashboard gives that person evidence to investigate.

## What the search research can tell you

[Pew Research Center's July 2025 analysis](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) used browsing data from **900 U.S. adults during March 2025**. Visits to Google results with an AI summary led to a traditional-result click **8%** of the time, compared with **15%** without a summary. Clicks on a link inside the summary occurred on **1%** of visits to pages with a summary.

That is a useful observation about that sample and period. It does not measure your site's traffic loss, establish that the summary caused the difference or describe all ChatGPT searches. I would use it as a reason to inspect your own acquisition channels, rather than a forecast for your business.

## Check access, cost and the work involved

As checked on **22 October 2026**, HubSpot advertises standalone [HubSpot AEO](https://www.hubspot.com/products/aeo) at **$50 USD per month**, before applicable tax. AEO is also included in Marketing Hub Professional and Enterprise. Features, permissions and usage limits differ between the standalone and Marketing Hub experiences; check the current packaging for your account.

The subscription is only part of the cost. Someone needs time to select prompts, inspect citations and improve content. If you already have access, I would still define that work before enabling a pilot.

HubSpot's free AEO Grader provides a one-time diagnostic. The paid AEO product supports ongoing tracking. Choose the level of measurement that answers your question.

## Make the existing information useful first

Start with clear pages about your expertise, the problems you work on and evidence for your claims. Answer practical buyer questions, keep product details current and make important information available as readable text. Explain what a case demonstrates and where its limits are.

For Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, [Google says existing SEO fundamentals still apply](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). It does not require special AI markup or a new machine-readable file. A page must be indexed and eligible for a search snippet to qualify as a supporting link; meeting the requirements does not guarantee inclusion.

I would fix inaccessible pages, unclear service descriptions and unsupported claims before producing more articles. Use a clear contact route and make sure enquiries reach someone who can respond. The [onboarding checks](/blog/hubspot-onboarding-mistakes) cover ownership and handovers inside HubSpot.

## Run a pilot around real buyer questions

Choose a small set of questions from sales conversations, support requests or the decisions your audience needs to make. Include unbranded questions and relevant comparisons. Check that the prompts use your buyers' language and location.

Record a baseline, keep the prompt set stable and inspect the answers and cited pages. HubSpot recommends reviewing [prompt performance over multiple days or weeks](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/seo/review-and-manage-aeo-prompts) because responses change. Note content changes and other campaigns so a change in visibility is not automatically credited to one edit.

Alongside mentions and citations, review identifiable referral visits, useful enquiries and what those visitors do next. Some discovery will not produce a trackable click. Record that measurement limit rather than treating every mention as a lead.

## Decide what the findings help you change

I would continue when the tracking reveals relevant gaps and the team can act on them. If the main findings are already-known content problems, address those before expanding the tool or publishing schedule.
