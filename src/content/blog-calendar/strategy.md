# Content Strategy -- Tom Schoorstra HubSpot Blog

Last updated: 2026-04-12

## Mission

Build topical authority in HubSpot operations for SMBs and scale-ups. Drive inbound leads for six services: automation, consultancy, integrations, custom objects, pipeline optimization, and custom app cards. Position Tom as the independent HubSpot contractor who writes honestly about what works and what doesn't.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** Operations leads, founders, and RevOps managers at SMBs (10-200 employees) who use or are evaluating HubSpot.
- **Secondary:** Marketing/sales managers tasked with "owning HubSpot" on top of their actual job.
- **Tertiary:** HubSpot admins looking for practical implementation guidance.

## Differentiators (vs. agency blogs)

1. **Independent contractor voice** -- honest, no upsell agenda, no fluff.
2. **HubSpot App Cards / UI Extensions** -- almost zero competitor content exists. October 2026 classic CRM card sunset creates urgency.
3. **Dutch market + English content** -- HubSpot + Exact Online integration content is a near-empty niche in English.
4. **Practitioner perspective on Breeze AI** -- most content is agency marketing; honest "what actually works" content is missing.
